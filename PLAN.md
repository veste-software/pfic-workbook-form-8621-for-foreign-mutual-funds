## Widen the engine

- [ ] **8. Mark-to-market (Part IV).** `src/lib/engine/mtm.ts`: for each lot and each year from E onward: gain = year-end value − adjusted basis, included as ordinary income. A loss is allowed only up to that lot's unreversed inclusions. Basis rises by included gains and falls by allowed losses. Lots bought during a year are marked at that year-end. A redemption gives ordinary gain, an ordinary loss up to unreversed inclusions, and a capital loss for the rest (shown separately). Form lines are the sums over lots. A US-person date inside a lot's holding period raises a flag. Check: `tests/engine/fixture-2c-mtm.test.ts` (3 years, one loss year limited by unreversed inclusions, basis carried) passes.
- [ ] **9. Late MTM election (§1296(j)).** In year E, lots held before Jan 1 of E go through task 6 as if sold at the year-E year-end value (Part V), and their MTM basis afterwards is that value. Lots bought in E are plain MTM. Part II election box in year E only. Check: `tests/engine/fixture-2d-late-election.test.ts` passes.
- [ ] **10. Excess distributions, flags and blocking.** `src/lib/engine/excess.ts`: lines 15a–15e (125% of the average distributions of the prior 3 years). A positive excess raises "allocation not supported in this version". `src/lib/engine/flags.ts` collects every flag from SPEC §5a, plus QEF selected ("not supported, see a preparer"). A missing year-end NAV or FX for a needed year blocks that fund's results with a message naming the year. Check: `tests/engine/fixture-2e-flags.test.ts` (the mixed gain/loss flag, the missing-NAV block, the excess-distribution flag) passes.
- [ ] **11. Engine facade.** `src/lib/engine/index.ts` `computeFund(fund, settings) → FundResult` (Part I, Part II, Part IV, Part V, per-lot rows, flags, blocked reason) and `computeWorkbook`. Wire the island to it for both regimes. Check: all engine fixture tests go through `computeFund`.
